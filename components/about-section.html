About TDM Group

A Unified Platform for Sustainable Progress

TDM Group is a diversified Indian conglomerate and fundamentally driven by a single vision: to ensure that progress, innovation, and social responsibility are intrinsically linked. Founded in 2021, the company is known for building a future where our commercial success directly fuels positive global impact and its businesses are spread across India and also have an international presence in Singapore.

Our Core Strength

Our core strength lies in IT and Media (TDM Networks & TD Media), where we deliver robust IT Solutions, Including Network, Cybersecurity and digital strategy, alongside creative media and marketing services businesses to thrive in the digital age—ensuring they are secure, visible, and technologically advanced.

We Invest in People

Our passion for human well-being guides our Healthcare sector, where we focus on delivering next-generation services and technology through Novo Robotics surgery centre to enhance patient care.

We Share Knowledge

Through our Podcast channel TDM Manthan, we foster essential industry conversations, inspire next generations, sharing valuable insights and thought leadership with the wider community.

We Commit to Impact

The TDM Foundation is the heart of our enterprise. It acts as our engine for positive change, channelling our collective resources into tangible initiatives focused on community development and education.

In short, TDM Group isn't just a collection of successful businesses; we are a unified platform for sustainable progress. We leverage the power of technology and media to support human health and drive meaningful societal change.
